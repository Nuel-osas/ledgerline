Prove this operator’s revenue. Watch their credit rise.

Proof submission is permissionless: there is no privileged relayer, so you can submit it with your own wallet. The registry authenticates the contract that emitted the revenue, not whoever brought the proof.

Try it yourself

0 / 5
  1. 1

    Connect a wallet

    Any wallet. You are not the borrower here: you are about to act as the keeper, which anyone can do.

    Use the Connect Wallet button above.

  2. 2

    Take some gas

    A relayer pushes 0.5 CTC to you. You sign nothing, because needing gas to ask for gas would defeat the point.

  3. 3

    Prove the operator’s revenue

    Pick any unproven settlement and submit its Attestcoin proof yourself. 0 are waiting. The 0x0FD2 precompile verifies it inside your transaction.

  4. 4

    Watch credit appear

    Once enough periods are proven, an unsecured limit appears, priced from run-rate and how many networks pay this operator.

  5. 5

    Draw against it

    Borrowing is scoped to whoever earned the revenue. Import the demo operator key at the bottom of this page to try it.

A period counts exactly once, across every transaction. Proving the same settlement twice reverts with Period already counted, which is the guard that stops one settlement being replayed into an unlimited credit limit.
Try every feature, including the draw.

Proving revenue works from any wallet, because submission is permissionless. Drawing is scoped to the borrower, because credit belongs to whoever earned it. So that the restriction does not stop you testing, here is the demo operator’s key. It is a throwaway on testnet and holds nothing of value anywhere.
